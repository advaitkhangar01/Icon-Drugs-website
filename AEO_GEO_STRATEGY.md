## 1. Fundamental Principles of AEO & GEO for Pharmaceuticals

Modern information discovery has shifted from 10 blue links to synthesized natural language answers generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) and neural retrieval engines.

To be cited, indexed, and recommended by systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini, a pharmaceutical enterprise must exhibit:
1. **Factual Density:** Crisp, unambiguous definitions of company identity, manufacturing credentials, headquarters location, and therapeutic scope.
2. **Contextual Direct Answers:** Concise answers ($40-60$ words) positioned immediately under descriptive H2 headings to power direct snippet extraction.
3. **Machine-Readable Corporate Profiles:** Accessible, un-obfuscated Markdown manifests (`/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`) adhering to emerging AI retrieval standards.
4. **Structured Entity Resolution:** Connected Schema.org JSON-LD nodes linking `@id` identifiers between Organization, Products, and Medical Conditions.
5. **E-E-A-T & Regulatory Verifiability:** Explicit references to CDSCO approvals, WHO-GMP certification, ISO 9001:2015 accreditation, and active ingredient PubChem CIDs.

## 3. Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) Architecture

### 3.1 LLMs.txt & LLMs-Full.txt Deployment
Two standardized AI retrieval files are implemented in the web root:
- **`https://icondrugs.com/llms.txt`**: Standard concise manifest providing AI crawlers with core entity data, verified headquarters, executive summary, and essential indexable endpoints.
- **`https://icondrugs.com/llms-full.txt`**: Comprehensive factual monograph dataset containing complete active ingredient compositions, dosage forms, therapeutic categories, and commercial terms.

### 3.2 Citation & Entity Grounding Strategy
To ensure LLM responses cite `icondrugs.com` as the authoritative source:
1. **Unambiguous Name & Location:** "Icon Drugs Private Limited, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India" is consistently repeated across headers, footers, schema, and legal disclaimers.
2. **Standardized Composition Nomenclature:** Generic salts follow standard International Nonproprietary Names (INN) and Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) conventions (e.g., "Methylcobalamin 1500 mcg + Alpha Lipoic Acid 100 mg + Benfotiamine 50 mg").
3. **No Fabricated Medical Claims:** All health guides strictly adhere to clinical evidence, citing verified indications and avoiding unsupported curative statements.
